🚗 Driver Safety (DVLA)
🚗 UK DVLA Guidance — Insulin-Treated Drivers
Before driving: Check BG ≥5 mmol/L. If 4.0–5.0 mmol/L, take 15–20g carbs and wait 45 min.

During journeys: Check every 2 hours minimum. Confirm with fingerprick if CGM shows low reading.

If hypo while driving: Stop safely, remove keys, leave driver's seat, treat, wait ≥45 min after recovery before driving.

DVLA notification required if: More than one severe hypo in 12 months, impaired hypo awareness, or any relevant changes. Failure to notify is a legal offence.

CGM: Useful for trend monitoring but finger-prick BG required to confirm a low before acting.
DVLA Guidance 2023
⚠️ Group 2 Licence (HGV/LGV) — Additional Requirements
Group 2 drivers on insulin must: check BG within 2 hrs before driving AND every hour during driving, keep records for DVLA inspection, meet stricter hypo unawareness criteria. Refer to DVLA specialist assessment. Inform insurer of insulin use.
DVLA Guidance 2023

🔤 Abbreviations & Glossary

All abbreviations used in this tool.

A
AGP
Ambulatory Glucose Profile — Standardised CGM report showing TIR, TBR, TAR, CV, and average glucose over 14 days.
AID
Automated Insulin Delivery — System automatically adjusting insulin in response to CGM readings. Also called closed-loop or artificial pancreas.
AIT
Active Insulin Time — Duration the system treats a bolus as still active. Shorter AIT = more aggressive correction. Also called DIA.
C
CGM
Continuous Glucose Monitor — Wearable sensor measuring interstitial glucose every 1–5 min.
CV%
Coefficient of Variation — (SD / Mean Glucose) × 100. Measure of glucose variability. Target: <36%. A high CV with acceptable TIR suggests unpredictable swings despite an acceptable average.
D
DIA
Duration of Insulin Action — See AIT.
DSN
Diabetes Specialist Nurse
DVLA
Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency — UK body that must be notified of relevant medical conditions affecting fitness to drive.
G
GMI
Glucose Management Indicator — Estimated HbA1c from CGM average. Formula: GMI(%) = 3.31 + 0.02392 × mean glucose (mg/dL).
H
HbA1c
Glycated Haemoglobin — Lab test reflecting average blood glucose over 2–3 months.
HCL
Hybrid Closed Loop — AID system automating basal insulin but requiring meal boluses (except iLet).
I
ICR
Insulin:Carb Ratio — Grams of carbohydrate covered by 1 unit of bolus insulin (e.g. 1:10 = 1 unit per 10g). Different ratios per meal are normal and expected.
IOB
Insulin on Board — Estimated active insulin remaining from previous boluses and automated doses.
ISF
Insulin Sensitivity Factor — How much 1 unit lowers blood glucose. Also called Correction Factor (CF).
T
TAR
Time Above Range — % of CGM readings above 10.0 mmol/L (180 mg/dL). Target: <25%.
TBR
Time Below Range — % of CGM readings below 3.9 mmol/L (70 mg/dL). Target: <4%. Address before targeting hyperglycaemia.
TDD
Total Daily Dose — Total insulin in 24 hrs (basal + bolus). Used to estimate ICR (500 ÷ TDD) and ISF (100 ÷ TDD for mmol/L).
TIR
Time in Range — % of CGM readings between 3.9–10.0 mmol/L (70–180 mg/dL). Target: >70%.
