🔤 Abbreviations & Glossary

All abbreviations used in this tool, listed alphabetically.

A
AGP
Ambulatory Glucose Profile — A standardised CGM report showing glucose patterns over 14 days (TIR, TBR, TAR at a glance).
AID
Automated Insulin Delivery — A system that automatically adjusts insulin delivery in response to CGM readings. Also called closed-loop or artificial pancreas.
AIT
Active Insulin Time — The duration the system treats a bolus as still active. Also called Duration of Insulin Action (DIA). Shorter AIT = more aggressive correction.
B
BG
Blood Glucose — The concentration of glucose in the blood, measured in mmol/L.
C
CF / Correction Factor
Correction Factor — See ISF. How much 1 unit of insulin lowers blood glucose (mmol/L per unit).
CGM
Continuous Glucose Monitor — A wearable sensor measuring interstitial glucose every 1–5 min, the core input for all AID systems.
D
DIA
Duration of Insulin Action — See AIT. Typically 2–6 hrs depending on the pump.
DSN
Diabetes Specialist Nurse — A nurse with specialist training in diabetes management and AID systems.
G
GMI
Glucose Management Indicator — An estimated HbA1c calculated from average CGM glucose over 14+ days.
H
HbA1c
Glycated Haemoglobin — Lab test reflecting average blood glucose over 2–3 months. Target typically <53 mmol/mol (<7%) for most adults with T1D.
HCL
Hybrid Closed Loop — An AID system automating basal insulin but still requiring meal boluses (except iLet).
HCP
Healthcare Professional — A clinician involved in the patient's diabetes care (DSN, diabetologist, GP).
I
ICR
Insulin:Carb Ratio — Grams of carbohydrate covered by 1 unit of bolus insulin (e.g. 1:10 = 1 unit per 10 g). Different ratios per meal are normal and expected.
IOB
Insulin on Board — Estimated active insulin remaining from previous bolus and automated doses. AID systems track IOB to avoid stacking.
ISF
Insulin Sensitivity Factor — How much 1 unit lowers blood glucose (mmol/L per unit). Also called Correction Factor (CF).
M
mmol/L
Millimoles per Litre — UK/European unit for blood glucose. Normal fasting range ~3.9–5.5 mmol/L.
T
TAR
Time Above Range — % of CGM readings above 10.0 mmol/L. Target: <25%.
TBR
Time Below Range — % of CGM readings below 3.9 mmol/L. Target: <4%. Address before targeting hyperglycaemia.
TDD
Total Daily Dose — Total insulin in 24 hrs (basal + bolus). Used to estimate ICR (500 ÷ TDD) and ISF (100 ÷ TDD). Target split ~50% basal / 50% bolus.
TIR
Time in Range — % of CGM readings between 3.9–10.0 mmol/L. Target: >70%.
